Body Eras Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Body Eras is an iPhone app for taking progress photos and viewing them alongside your Apple Health stats. It is built so that your data stays with you. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any of your data.

Photos and check-ins

Photos, notes, and the health stats saved with each check-in are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Body Eras removes location information from photos when it saves them. A photo is saved to your Photos library only when you choose Export.

Apple Health

With your permission, Body Eras reads weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass, waist circumference, resting heart rate, and VO2 max from Apple Health. It uses this information only to show it in the app. Body Eras never writes to Apple Health, and Health data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties. You can change or remove access at any time in the Health app.

iCloud

If you turn on iCloud sync (when available), your check-ins are stored in your own private iCloud account, managed by Apple. We cannot access your iCloud data.

Camera, Face ID, and notifications

The camera is used only to take your progress photos. Face ID or your passcode is used only to unlock the app if you turn on the app lock; Body Eras never receives your biometric data. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

No accounts, analytics, or ads

Body Eras has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code that collects data.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual check-ins, or everything at once in Settings › Delete all check-ins. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

Body Eras is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
